# 口语 Part 3 方法论

## 一、分维度讨论思路

### 1. 按性格划分（话题短语）

**Well, it really depends on different personalities**

- **内向型人（introverted people）**
  - 特征：shy（害羞）、timid（胆怯）、clumsy in socializing（不擅长社交）、have social phobia（社交恐惧）、keep silent（沉默）、avoid eye contact（避免眼神接触）、keep a low profile（低调）
  - 行为：stay at home（宅家）、read books（读书）、listen to music（听音乐）、watch short videos（看短视频）、prefer online communication（偏好线上交流）、spend time alone（独处）
  - 感受：feel comfortable and relaxed by being alone（独处时舒适放松）、stay in comfort zone（待在舒适区）、dislike catching people’s eyes（不喜欢被关注）、feel exhausted and uneasy by face-to-face talk（面对面交流感到疲惫不安）
- **外向型人（extroverted people）**
  - 特征：outgoing（外向）、easygoing（随和）、talkative（健谈）、energetic（精力充沛）、party animals（派对达人）、social butterflies（社交达人）、not afraid of making a big scene（不怕引人注目）
  - 行为：outdoor activities（户外活动）、join/hold parties（参加 / 举办派对）、socialize with others in person（线下社交）、step out of comfort zone（走出舒适区）、attract people’s attention（吸引关注）、meet new friends（结交新朋友）、share feelings directly（直接分享感受）
  - 感受：recharge their batteries by interacting with others（通过社交恢复精力）、enjoy being part of the crowd（享受融入群体）、like being under the spotlight（喜欢成为焦点）

### 2. 情绪价值

**核心关键词**：emotional value（情绪价值）、emotional supports（情感支持）、emotional connections（情感联结）、sentimental value（情感价值）、mental supports（心理支持）

- **正面情绪**：happiness（快乐）、achievement（成就感）、accomplishment（成就感）、satisfaction（满足感）、fulfillment（充实感）、motivation（动力）、confidence（自信）
- **负面情绪**：anxiety（焦虑）、depression（抑郁）、loneliness（孤独）、isolation（孤立）、boredom（无聊）、tiredness（疲惫）、sadness（悲伤）

**常用表达**：

1. **can provide emotional values to / can gain emotional values from**
2. **to satisfy their emotional needs**
3. **help them to have a better mental condition**
4. **beneficial for their mental health**        -- motivated, motivation,confident, confidence

### 6. 特定群体分析

#### （1）孩子（children/kids/students）

- 缺点：make noise to express feelings（通过吵闹表达情绪）、frequent emotional changes（情绪多变）、cry（哭闹）、annoying/disturbing（令人困扰）、impatient（没耐心）、immature（不成熟）、easily distracted/influenced（易分心 / 受影响）、cannot distinguish right from wrong（无法分辨对错）、lack critical thinking/problem-solving skills（缺乏批判性思维 / 解决问题能力）、lack self-control/self-discipline（缺乏自控 / 自律）、rely on parents（依赖父母）、weak/vulnerable（脆弱）、easily cheated/injured（易受骗 / 受伤）
- 优点：active（活泼）、positive（积极）、energetic（有活力）、enthusiastic（热情）、adventurous（爱冒险）、cute（可爱）、creative（有创造力）、imaginative（有想象力）、curious（好奇）、easily motivated/satisfied（易被激励 / 满足）
- 需求：need guidance/supervision（需要引导 / 监督）、need protection（需要保护）、develop right value systems/good habits（培养正确价值观 / 好习惯）

#### （2）父母（parents）

- 角色：first teacher of kids（孩子的第一任老师）、supervisor（监督者）、role model（榜样）、set an example for kids（为孩子树立榜样）、kids copy behaviors from parents（孩子模仿父母行为）、set reward and punishment systems（设立奖惩机制）、parenting skills（育儿技巧）、avoid spoiling kids（避免宠坏孩子）

#### （3）老师（teachers）

- 特点：professional（专业）、systematical（系统的）、academic（学术的）、comprehensive（全面的）
- 作用：develop students' right value systems/behaviors/habits（培养学生正确价值观 / 行为 / 习惯）

### 7. 万能技能法（8 点）

1. **Time-management skills**（时间管理能力）：timetable（时间表）、schedule（计划）、to-do list（待办清单）、punctual（准时）、improve work/study efficiency（提高效率）
2. **Money-management/money-making skills**（理财 / 赚钱能力）：save/earn money（存钱 / 赚钱）、make a living（谋生）、satisfy material needs（满足物质需求）、get a foothold in society（在社会立足）
3. **Problem-solving/decision-making skills**（解决问题 / 决策能力）：accumulate life experience（积累经验）、learn from others（向他人学习）、make quick and right decisions（快速正确决策）
4. **Critical/independent thinking skills**（批判性 / 独立思考能力）：think from different perspectives（多角度思考）、objective（客观）、empathy（同理心）、creativity（创造力）、imagination（想象力）、right value systems（正确价值观）
5. **Self-study skills**（自学能力）：no supervision（无需监督）、self-motivated（自我激励）、internal motivation（内在动力）、concentration（专注力）
6. **Communication/interpersonal skills**（沟通 / 人际交往能力）：share feelings（分享感受）、make new friends（结交朋友）、accumulate human resources（积累人脉）、start a conversation（开启对话）、find common topics（找到共同话题）
7. **Teamwork/cooperation skills**（团队合作能力）：exchange ideas（交流想法）、brainstorming（头脑风暴）、generate new ideas（产生新想法）、understand strengths and weaknesses（了解优缺点）
8. **Life skills**（生活技能）：cooking（烹饪）、housework（家务）、technology operation（技术操作）、language（语言）、repairing（维修）

### 1. Describe an interesting traditional story

You should say:

- What the story is about
- When/how you knew it
- Who told you the story
- And explain how you felt when you first heard it

One of the most famous traditional stories I’ve heard is *Journey to the West*. It is a Chinese classic about a monk named Xuanzang, who travels to India to bring back Buddhist scriptures. He is joined by four disciples, including the well-known Monkey King, Sun Wukong.

I first heard this story when I was young, from my grandparents. They would often tell me about how Sun Wukong, with his magical powers and wild behavior, learned to be more patient and kind as he traveled with the monk. The story is full of adventure, magic, and valuable lessons.

What I remember most about the story is how Sun Wukong changes. At the beginning, he is rebellious and doesn’t follow rules, but as he faces many challenges, he learns to be humble, work with others, and never give up. These are important lessons that I think about when I face difficulties in my own life.

The story is also special because it teaches the importance of staying strong and having faith, no matter how tough things get. It shows that even people who make mistakes can change and become better. Sun Wukong and the other characters have become very important in Chinese culture, and *Journey to the West* is still loved by many people today.

Looking back now, I realize that the toy symbolized my father’s love and care. It wasn’t just entertainment—it was a reminder of the effort he put in to make me happy. Even today, whenever I think about it, I feel a strong sense of warmth and gratitude.
