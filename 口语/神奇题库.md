### Part 1

1. **Machine**
   - Which machine in your home do you use most often?
   - Do you think washing machines and sweeping machines are important?
   - Do you read the instructions before you use a machine for the first time?
   - Do you think it is important to read the instructions?
   - Have you ever used a sewing machine to make clothes?
   - What do you do when a machine in your home breaks down?
2. **Phone**
   - What do you usually use your phone for?
   - Have you ever lost or broken your phone?
   - Would you be happy to receive a phone as a present?
   - Do you think it's good to stop people using their phones in certain places?
3. **Crowded place**
   - Where in your city are there usually a lot of people?
   - When was the last time you went to a crowded place?
   - Do you like staying in crowded places?
   - How do you feel when you are in a crowded place?
4. **Praise**
   - How do you feel when someone says that you've done something well?
   - Have you told anyone recently that they did something well?
   - Do you remember a teacher telling you that you'd done something really well?
   - Is it important for parents to tell children when they do things well?
   - Have you received any praise recently?
5. **Memory**
   - What sort of things do you find difficult to remember?
   - What do you do when you need to remember something important?
   - Did you find it easier to remember things when you were a child?
   - Do you think having a good memory will be important in your future job or studies?
6. **Borrow**
   - Do you borrow books from your friends?
   - Do you lend things to others?
   - Will you lend your mobile phone to others?
   - What do you think about borrowing money from friends?
7. **Shoes**
   - Do you like buying shoes? How often?
   - Have you ever bought shoes online?
   - How much money do you usually spend on shoes?
   - Which do you prefer, fashionable shoes or comfortable shoes?
8. **Patience**
   - Are you usually a patient person?
   - What is it that makes you feel impatient?
   - How do you feel when you have to wait for something?
   - Do you need to be patient in your work or studies?
   - Were you more or less patient when you were younger?
9. **Photograph**
   - How often do you take photos?
   - Do you ever print the photos that you have taken?
   - Do your family and friends take a lot of photos of themselves?
   - Would you like to be a professional photographer?
   - Do you like to take photographs?
10. **Advertisement**
    - Do you often see advertisements on your phone or computer?
    - What is the most impressive advertisement you have seen?
    - Are there a lot of advertisements around you?
    - Do you think advertisements are useful?
11. **Flowers**
    - Do you love flowers?
    - Do you know anyone who really likes flowers?
    - Are there many flowers growing near where you live?
    - Have you ever taken a photo of flowers?
    - Do people often give each other flowers in your country?
12. **Being busy**
    - Is your life busier now than when you were younger?
    - When was the last time you were busy?
    - Are you busy with your work or studies at the moment?
    - Do you like to be busy when you're on holiday?
    - Which do you think is worse: being too busy, or not having enough to do?
13. **Fruit and vegetable**
    - Are you interested in growing fruits and vegetables?
    - Do you think plants are easy to grow?
    - Is the place where you live suitable for growing fruits and vegetables?
    - Do you watch TV programs about fruits and vegetables?
14. **Public places**
    - Have you ever talked to a stranger in a public place?
    - Do you usually wear headphones when you're in public places?
    - Do you think we need more public places?
15. **Staying up**
    - Do you often stay up late at night?
    - Did you stay up late more often when you were younger?
    - What do you usually do when you stay up late?
    - How do you feel the day after you have stayed up late?
16. **Cooking**
    - How often do you cook for other people?
    - Would you enjoy cooking for a large group of people?
    - Do you think working as a chef in a restaurant is a good job?
    - Would you like to learn more about cooking?
17. **Museum**
    - Do you like going to museums?
    - How often do you go to museums?
    - Do you think more museums should be built in your city?
    - Did you often go to museums when you were a child?
    - Do you like going to museums with your friends?
18. **Rules**
    - Do you like rules?
    - Did you have any strict teachers when you were a child?
    - Are there any rules for students in your school?
    - Do you think schools should have more rules or fewer rules? Why?
